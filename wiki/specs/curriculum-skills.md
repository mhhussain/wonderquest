# Spec: Curriculum & Skills (GSRP readiness)

**Purpose**: What the app teaches and how it maps to readiness goals.
**Status**: prototype-validated (coverage) + spec (gaps).

## Target skills (from brief)
Letter recognition (upper+lower), letter sounds/phonics, number recognition, counting, early math, fine motor, pattern recognition, problem solving, science, geography, curiosity, focus/independent learning. Plus parent additions: **reading (phonics + word building)**, **Arabic letters**.

## Pedagogy principles baked in
- Objects/visuals **before** symbols (math reveals equation after solving).
- **Confusable-letter families** for discrimination (b/d, p/q; Arabic dot families).
- Short rounds, immediate reward (15–20 min focus window).
- Lowercase is the explicit headline skill.
- Audio narration for pre-readers; tracing for fine motor + letter formation.
